# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this repository to be a respectful, useful place for users, contributors, and maintainers.

## Expected behavior

Please:

- be respectful
- assume good intent
- give actionable feedback
- keep technical disagreement professional
- focus criticism on code, design, and behavior rather than people

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples include:

- harassment or abuse
- personal attacks
- discriminatory language
- bad-faith hostility
- deliberate disruption of discussion or contribution flow

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, issues, PRs, or contributions that violate this code of conduct.

## Reporting

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers through the repository’s private reporting path.
